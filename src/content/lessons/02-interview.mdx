Vibe will start by asking how you want to move through the course: fast track (blaze through, no quizzes, short answers) or thorough (quizzes, fuller explanations). Fast track is the default, so if you skip this question, or the whole interview, that's what you get.

If you go fast track, Vibe asks one more optional, free-form question about your background, or you can type "skip" and get started right away. If you go thorough, Vibe asks a few multiple choice questions about your experience, the Vibe surfaces you want to learn, and how you like to learn. All of them are optional.

Vibe also detects your operating system and, when it can, which surface you're using, so you don't have to tell it.

If you picked a role on the homepage (developer, team lead, or knowledge worker), Vibe already knows it and won't ask again.

You can change any answer later just by asking Vibe to update it.
