Vibe Work is the other half of Vibe: the mode for everything that isn't a codebase. You describe an outcome, and Work gathers context from files, the web, and your connected tools, breaks the job into steps, shows you each one, and asks before it does anything you'd want to check. It lives at chat.mistral.ai, on mobile, and in Vibe Desktop.

## Work or Code

| Pick | When the task |
|---|---|
| **Work** | Needs several steps or sources, actions in tools like Gmail, Slack, or Notion, or a document you'll review |
| **Code** | Depends on a codebase, terminal, IDE, or coding session (see the [Surfaces](/lessons/surfaces) lesson) |

Work is also where quick conversations happen now. You choose how deep Vibe goes: **fast** for quick replies, **think** for deeper, agentic work. Depending on your account, this is a fast/think toggle or a reasoning level in the **Choose model** menu next to the input.

## Start a task

A good prompt says what you want, which sources to use, who it's for, and any limits:

```text
Research this topic and create a one-page brief I can share with my team.
```

```text
Compare these two documents and highlight the main differences.
```

Work picks tools and context from your prompt. You can also point it at a source yourself: [connectors](/lessons/connectors), [Libraries](/lessons/projects), [skills](/lessons/automations), uploaded [files](/lessons/canvas), or the web.

## Follow the todos

For longer tasks, Work shows a live **todos panel** on the right: a checklist of steps it has done or is about to do. There's no plan to approve upfront; it starts right away. Alongside, you see:

- **Clarifying questions** when your prompt is ambiguous. Pick an answer and it continues.
- A **reasoning summary** for each step: what it's reading, comparing, and ruling out.
- Every **tool call** (for example `Web Search` or `Gmail: List Messages`) with its inputs, outputs, and status. Expand one to check it used the right source.

Click **stop** (the black square) if it goes the wrong way, then redirect with a follow-up message. Quick one-shot tasks skip the todos panel and answer directly.

## Approvals

Before Work creates, modifies, sends, posts, or deletes anything in an external tool, it stops and asks:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Continue** | Approve this one action. It asks again next time. |
| **Always allow** | Pre-authorize this function for the session |
| **Decline** | Cancel the action; Work skips it or asks how to proceed |

Read recipients, content, and destination before you click `Continue`. Stop may not catch an action that already reached the other system.

For finer control, open `Connectors` in the sidebar, then the `My connectors` tab and a connector. Its page lists each function with a permission menu (such as `Ask` or `Allow`). Pre-allow read-only ones you use often, and keep sends and deletes on manual approval. These choices are per user. The [Connectors](/lessons/connectors) lesson goes deeper.

## Web search and Open URL

Web search is built in: ask for current information and Work searches on its own, showing **Ran a web search** steps you can expand. When Work uses it, the answer shows a **globe icon**, inline links, and a `Sources` button listing every reference. News queries draw on AFP and AP.

With web search on, paste a URL (or several) into your message and Work reads the page; you'll see an `Opened Page` mention. It reads only that page, and can't get past logins or paywalls. For a long, cited report, use the **Deep Research** skill.

## Voice

Click the microphone icon at the bottom right of the chat and speak. With `Autosend` on, your words are sent straight away; with it off, the transcript lands in the message box so you can edit it first.

## Organizations and workspaces

Click your **Organization name** in the bottom-left corner to switch Workspace or Organization. Chat history, projects, skills, scheduled tasks, custom instructions, and connector sign-ins are all scoped per Workspace, and switching affects every open tab.

## Local folders in Desktop

In Vibe Desktop, Work can use folders on your machine. In the composer, click **+**, open **Folders**, and choose **Add a folder**. Add as many as you need, then explain what to do with them. **+ → Folders → Remove all** clears them. Adding a folder makes it available; it doesn't mean every file has been read.

## Coming from Chat

Chat and Work are now one experience. Here's where the old features went:

| Chat (legacy) | Now |
|---|---|
| Agents | [Skills](/lessons/automations): type `/` in the input to pick one, or mention it by name |
| Think mode | The fast/think toggle, or a reasoning level in the model menu, depending on your account |
| Deep Research | The Deep Research skill |
| Code Interpreter | Native [Code Interpreter](/lessons/canvas) in Work (paid plans) |
| Memories | [Knowledge](/lessons/projects) |

Learn more in [Get started with Work](https://docs.mistral.ai/vibe/work/web/get-started) and [Safety and approvals](https://docs.mistral.ai/vibe/work/web/safety-and-approvals).
