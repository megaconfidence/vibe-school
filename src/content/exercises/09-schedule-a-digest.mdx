Some questions come back every week: what changed in the repo, what's in my inbox, what's new in my field. In this exercise, you'll write that question once and let Vibe Work answer it on a schedule.

## Pick a digest

| Digest | Uses |
|---|---|
| Weekly summary of new and stale issues in one repository | GitHub App [connector](/glossary#connector) |
| Daily rundown of unread email from your manager and key clients | Gmail or Outlook connector |
| Monday roll-up of what happened in two Slack channels | Slack connector |
| Weekly news watch on a topic | Web search, no connector needed |

Start read-only. A digest that summarizes is safe to run unattended. One that sends or posts needs more care.

## Connect and test

1. Open the `Connectors` page from the sidebar, find the card, click `Connect`, and sign in. A green `Connected` indicator confirms it.
2. In a normal Work chat, run your prompt by hand. If Work doesn't pick the connector on its own, click `+`, open `Connectors`, and choose it. Web search runs automatically.

Make the prompt self-contained, because nobody will be there to clarify it:

```text
Summarize GitHub issues in acme/website opened or updated in the last 7 days.
Group them as: new, needs triage (no labels), and stale (no activity in 30 days).
For each, give the title, a link, and one line on what it's about. Keep it under 300 words.
```

Iterate until the output is what you'd want to read on a Monday morning.

## Schedule it

Either ask in the chat:

```text
Schedule this to run every Monday at 9am.
```

Work proposes a schedule and you confirm. Or create it from the sidebar:

1. Click `Scheduled`, then `New task`, and write the prompt.
2. Under **Model and reasoning**, keep the default or pick a model.
3. Under **Schedule**, pick a frequency (`Once`, `Daily`, `Weekly`, `Monthly`, or `Yearly`) and a time.
4. Under **Permissions**, keep `Follow connector settings` (the safe default), or pick connectors that may run without asking for this task.
5. Save the task.

Recurring tasks may start up to 60 minutes after their scheduled time.

<div class="callout">
A scheduled task runs while you're away, so an action that waits for approval would stall it. Keep digests read-only. To pre-authorize a specific function, open <code>Connectors</code>, then the <code>My connectors</code> tab, click the connector, and set the permission menu for that function only (for example <code>Allow</code>).
</div>

## After it runs

The result shows up in the sidebar with an **unread dot**. Open it to read the digest, ask follow-up questions, or jump to the associated chat to refine the prompt. From the `Scheduled` page you can edit, pause, or delete the task.

## Tips

- **Name the time window.** "In the last 7 days" beats "recently".
- **Fix the format.** The same headings every week make the digest skimmable.
- **Watch your plan.** The number of scheduled tasks you can have at once depends on your plan.

Scheduled tasks are in Public Preview, so names and options may change. More in the [Automations](/lessons/automations) and [Connectors](/lessons/connectors) lessons, and the [scheduled tasks docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/vibe/work/web/scheduled-tasks).
